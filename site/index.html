Your OBS mic chain, without OBS.

A Windows tray app that runs the mic filter chain you tuned in OBS Studio and sends it to a virtual audio cable. Discord, Zoom and games get the same processed mic while OBS stays closed.

Setup

From nothing to a processed mic in Discord, in four steps. Follow them in order, or skip what you already have.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Install OBS and a virtual cable

    OBS Studio 32.2.x and a virtual cable, on Windows 64-bit.

    knobs reads the mic and filters from your OBS settings, so OBS has to be installed and the mic tuned in it. It sends the result to a virtual cable, which other apps use as a mic.

    • OBS Studio 32.2.x obsproject.com
    • VB-Cable vb-audio.com

      Restart Windows after installing it.

    What knobs needs

    Windows
    64-bit
    OBS Studio
    32.2.x, tested on 32.2.2. knobs finds an install from OBS’s installer by itself; for a Steam or portable OBS, you choose its folder. Support for 33.0 follows its release.
    Virtual cable
    VB-Cable, or another one: knobs recognizes VB-Audio’s cables (VB-Cable, VoiceMeeter, Hi-Fi Cable) and Virtual Audio Cable.
    Mic filters
    OBS’s built-in audio filters: Noise Suppression (RNNoise or Speex), Noise Gate, Expander, Upward Compressor, Compressor, Limiter, Gain, 3-Band Equalizer and Invert Polarity. Known limits has the exceptions.
  2. 2

    Step 2: Tune your mic in OBS

    Add filters to your mic in OBS. If it’s already tuned, there’s nothing to do.

    If your mic is already set up in OBS, there’s nothing to do here. knobs uses the mic and filters in OBS’s active profile and scene collection, and if OBS monitors the mic to a cable, knobs sends to the same cable.

    New to OBS? Set up the mic in six steps

    You only need the mic, not a scene, a stream or a recording.

    1. Open OBS.
    2. In Settings › Audio, set Mic/Auxiliary Audio to your mic, if it isn’t already.
    3. In the Audio Mixer, click Mic/Aux and choose Filters.
    4. Under Audio Filters, click + and add filters as needed. They run from the top of the list down.
    5. Click each filter and adjust it by ear, starting from OBS’s defaults.
    6. Close OBS. knobs reads your filters when OBS closes.

    To hear the filters while you adjust them, choose headphones as the Monitoring Device in Settings › Audio, then set the mic’s Audio Monitoring to Monitor Only (mute output) in Edit › Advanced Audio Properties.

    What each audio filter does
    FilterWhat it does
    Noise SuppressionReduces steady background noise, such as a fan or hiss. RNNoise sounds better and uses more CPU than Speex.
    Noise GateSilences the mic while its level is below a threshold, such as between words.
    ExpanderTurns quiet sounds down, more gently than a gate.
    Upward CompressorTurns quiet sounds up.
    CompressorTurns loud sounds down past a threshold, so the level stays more even.
    LimiterKeeps the level from going over a threshold.
    GainTurns the level up or down.
    3-Band EqualizerTurns the low, mid and high frequencies up or down.
    Invert PolarityFlips the waveform upside down.

    OBS’s Filters Guide has more on each.

      Build it from source

      You need Visual Studio 2022 or newer with the Desktop development with C++ workload, and CMake 3.25 or newer. From a Developer PowerShell for VS:

      git clone https://github.com/danielalyoshin/knobs.git
cd knobs
cmake --preset x64
cmake --build --preset release

      Then run build\x64\Release\knobs.exe. The README has the full instructions and the tests.

      Start knobs

      Its first run checks what it found in OBS and asks only what it needs to:

      • Which mic, if OBS has several.
      • Which cable, if OBS doesn’t monitor the mic to one. With no cable installed, it links to VB-Cable and waits for one.
      • Filters it can’t run, if there are any. Known limits lists them.

      If you choose I’m new to OBS, it shows the steps above instead, and carries on by itself when you close OBS. The last page shows your chain, with Start with Windows checked.

      knobs then lives in the tray. Windows 11 hides new tray icons, so click ^ (Show hidden icons) on the taskbar to find the knob.

    • 4

      Step 4: Choose the cable in your apps

      The cable’s recording side, as the mic in Discord, Zoom and games.

      In Discord, Zoom, your games and your browser, choose the cable’s recording side as your mic. For VB-Cable, that’s CABLE Output.

      Turn off the app’s own processing

      Apps can process your mic again on top of your chain. Discord, for one, has its own noise suppression, echo cancellation and automatic gain control in Voice & Video. Turn them off to hear exactly what you tuned.

      To change your sound later, open OBS, change the filters and close it. knobs pauses while OBS is open, runs the new chain when it closes, and says what changed in a notification.

    How it works

    If you tuned your mic in OBS and send it to a virtual cable, OBS has to stay open all day. Other tools, such as Equalizer APO, VoiceMeeter and NVIDIA Broadcast, process audio their own way, so a chain tuned in OBS can’t match exactly. knobs runs OBS’s own filter code, from your OBS install, with your OBS settings. Nothing else touches the audio.

    OBS’s own filter code
    knobs ships no audio processing of its own. It loads OBS’s obs.dll, win-wasapi and obs-filters from a private copy of your OBS install, so its output is bit-identical to OBS’s.
    Out of OBS’s way
    knobs pauses while OBS is open, so the cable never gets your mic twice. When OBS closes, knobs reads its settings again and runs whatever you changed.
    In the tray, from sign-in
    knobs starts with Windows and lives in the tray. In testing, the mic was on the cable 9.5 s after signing in, with nothing clicked. A notification says when something needs you.
    Read-only on OBS
    knobs never writes to OBS’s settings or its install. The install stays unlocked, so a running knobs never blocks an OBS update.

    Known limits

    Check these before you download, so knobs does what you expect.

    One mic.
    With several mics in OBS, knobs runs the one you choose.
    No VST filters.
    knobs leaves them out of the chain and warns you.
    No NVIDIA noise removal.
    It’s in a separate OBS module, nv-filters, which knobs doesn’t load. The filter passes audio through unchanged, with a warning.
    No sidechain.
    knobs runs no other sources, so it runs the compressor as OBS does while the sidechain is silent, and warns you.
    Mute and push-to-talk don’t silence the cable.
    OBS’s monitoring ignores them too. The first run says so.
    The cable is quiet while OBS is open,
    unless OBS monitors your mic to it. Turn off Pause while OBS is open to keep knobs running then.
    A new sample rate or channel layout in OBS restarts knobs,
    as it restarts OBS, with a moment of silence.
    Windows only, and OBS 32.2.x only,
    for now.
