# Design System: knobs

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Red Caps on a Grey Desk"**

The page is a mixing desk seen from above: a near-neutral graphite ground (a cool pale grey desk in light), ink for the words, and red caps on the few things that matter. Cap red is the action colour and the colour of everything that is knobs: the download buttons, the recommended file's edge, knobs's bars in the charts, knobs's stage in the signal path, step 3's node, link underlines, hover and focus, and the cap dots that mark lists. Grounds, text and lines stay neutral, so the red has something to sing against. It is an open-source download page played straight: a hero band with a big condensed headline and one big versioned button, then a real download section with a row per file and its checksum, then the setup, the mechanism, the proof and the answers.

Sections are bands of tone, not cards: graphite, then the deep ground, alternating, and the footer as a band of cap red deep in both schemes. Structure is drawn with 1px rules, ruled rows and square panels; depth comes from tone, never from shadow. Every rectangle is square-cornered and every round thing is a true circle, because the mark is cut straight and only its circles are round. The page is dark for every visitor, whatever their system's scheme, and a toggle in the header switches it to light and remembers the choice.

Motion is quiet and singular: one curve, 160ms for feedback and 320ms for structure, the charts' one sweep, the signal path's one soft pass, and nothing else that moves on its own. A scheme switch lands in one frame. The page refuses gradients, glows, shadows for depth, glass, status colours and decoration beyond the cap dot.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Cap red is knobs and "press here", and nothing else; never an error, a warning or a success.
- Two schemes on one set of token names: graphite in dark, a cool pale grey desk with white panels in light.
- Sections as alternating bands of tone, closed by a cap red deep footer band in both schemes.
- Square corners on every rectangle; true circles only for round things.
- Radio Canada Condensed Bold for the few big headings, Radio Canada for every other word, Source Code Pro only for what can be copied.
- One curve, two durations, three motions that play once each.

## Colors

A cool graphite family on hue 260 for every ground, line and word, and one red, cap red, in shades of its own hue. All colours come from the knobs-brand tokens (`knobs-brand/tokens/tokens.json`, built to `dist/tokens.css`, which the deploy copies to `site/tokens.css`): the dark scheme on `:root`, the light scheme in two identical blocks (one under `prefers-color-scheme: light` without `data-theme="dark"`, one for `data-theme="light"`). Each page's `<html>` carries `data-theme="dark"`, so only the second applies, once the visitor chooses light. In the frontmatter a `-light` suffix marks the light scheme's value of the same CSS custom property (`ground-light` is `--ground` in light); it is not a separate token.

### Primary
- **Cap Red** (`action`, `action-light`): in dark the brand's cap red for dark grounds, in light cap red deep. Fills the solid buttons, outlines the secondary ones, draws the recommended file's 2px edge, knobs's bars, knobs's stage icon and panel edge, step 3's node, the cap dots, the fader handles, link underlines and the caret colour. As a fill or a line it holds at least 3:1 against its grounds (5.2:1 on graphite in dark, 4.5:1 on white in light).
- **Cap Red, Lit** (`action-hover`, `action-hover-light`): the hovered solid button. In dark it lights a step; in light it presses a step deeper.
- **Red Words** (`action-text`, `action-text-light`): red text, for a hovered link, an outlined button's label, the Recommended tag. In dark it equals cap red; in light it is a deeper shade so red words keep 4.5:1 on every light ground.
- **Words on Red** (`on-action`, `on-action-light`): graphite on cap red in dark, white on cap red deep in light.
- **Selection** (`selection`, `selection-light`): selected text's ground, a dark red in dark, a pale red in light.
- **Focus** (`focus`, `focus-light`): the 2px focus ring, cap red.

### Secondary
- **Cap Red Deep Band** (`band`): the footer's ground in both schemes, the brand's red for light grounds. Set once on `:root`; the light scheme keeps it.
- **Band Roles** (`on-band`, `band-line`, `band-line-strong`, `band-selection`, `band-focus`): inside the footer the page's roles take these on screen: every word, link and underline white (4.5:1), hairlines and strong rules in lighter shades of the band's red, a deeper red for selected text, a white focus ring.

### Neutral
- **Graphite** (`ground`): the page and the hero band in dark. **Pale Grey Desk** (`ground-light`): the same in light, cool, never warm or cream.
- **Deep Ground** (`ground-deep`, `ground-deep-light`): the alternate bands (Download for Windows with the steps, Benchmarks, Get knobs) and the preview strip.
- **Well** (`sunken`, `sunken-light`): wells inside panels: the SHA-256 and PowerShell values, the filter chain in knobs's panel.
- **Panel** (`raised`, `raised-light`): file rows, the release note panel, the build commands, knobs's panel. White in light.
- **Hairline** (`line`, `line-light`) and **Strong Rule** (`line-strong`, `line-strong-light`): row and table rules; control borders, section-opening rules, the step rail, tick zero lines.
- **Ink** (`text`, `text-light`): headings, emphasis, link words. Brand ink in dark, brand graphite in light.
- **Secondary Ink** (`text-2`, `text-2-light`): body copy, the lede, descriptions.
- **Quiet Ink** (`text-3`, `text-3-light`): labels, captions, notes, footnotes, the wire in the signal path.
- **Unlit Meter** (`chart-obs`, `chart-obs-light`): OBS's bars, an unlit grey beside knobs's lit red.
- **Defined, not yet used:** `--raised-2` (#25282c in dark, #f4f6f8 in light, commented as a panel's hover or a pressed control) is in `tokens.css` but no rule uses it; its role is open until the shared brand token set is decided. `--screen` (#131518 in dark, white in light) is the brand's frame for a walkthrough or a screenshot; the page has neither, and the reel, a brand film, sits on graphite with no frame. Both are left out of the frontmatter for that reason.

### Named Rules
**The Red Is knobs Rule.** Cap red marks knobs or "press here", and nothing else. It is never an error, a warning or a success: a pre-release tag stays grey, a copied check turns ink, and the page has no green or amber at all.

**The Words on Red Rule.** Words on cap red are graphite in dark and white in light (and white on the footer band); never brand ink on cap red. Small words on any red (a button's second line, the footer's legal lines) take weight 500, because white on cap red deep is 4.5:1 with no margin.

**The One Large Field Rule.** Red is edges, fills of controls, dots and bars. The footer band is the page's one large field of red, and it is a red the page already uses.

**The Unlit Meter Rule.** In the charts knobs is lit red and OBS an unlit grey. The light-mode chart colours stay as they are: there knobs's red and OBS's grey sit at nearly the same lightness, so labels, order, printed values and knobs's 600 weight carry the meaning, and colour is never the only thing that tells the bars apart.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Radio Canada Condensed Bold (falling back to Radio Canada, then a metric-matched Arial)
**Body Font:** Radio Canada, 400, 500 and 600 (falling back to "Radio Canada Fallback", Arial scaled to its width and line metrics)
**Mono Font:** Source Code Pro, 400, ligatures off

**Character:** a narrow, confident display cut for a handful of big headings over a plain, friendly sans that does all the reading. Every face carries Radio Canada's line metrics (ascent 94.5%, descent 25.5%, no line gap), so mono sits in prose without moving the line. Faces are self-hosted, Latin-1 subsets, `font-display: swap`; the first screen's four faces are preloaded and Source Code Pro waits.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Condensed 700, `clamp(2.25rem, 5.84vw, 5.5rem)`, 0.9): the hero headline only, 84px at 1440. Each phrase breaks as a whole. Short desktop windows step it down (4.5rem, then 3.25rem); below 900px it centres at `clamp(2.125rem, 1.2rem + 5.2vw, 3.75rem)`, 2rem below 360px. Forced colours set its line height to 1.
- **Headline** (Condensed 700, `clamp(2.125rem, 1.25rem + 2.2vw, 3.25rem)`, 1.02): section titles, hung 0.05em into the margin so their stems stand on the headline's left edge.
- **Headline Small** (Condensed 700, `clamp(1.875rem, 1.25rem + 1.4vw, 2.5rem)`, 1.02): Get knobs's title, a step under the section titles, hung the same way.
- **Footer Head** (Condensed 700, 1.375rem, 1.1): the footer's column heads, hung the same way.
- **Title** (600, 1.25rem, 1.3): a file row's title, a benchmark's claim; About these downloads at 1.375rem, and the checksums file's quieter row at 1rem.
- **Title Small** (600, 1.1875rem, 1.3 to 1.35): step titles, the facts, the questions.
- **Lede** (400, 1.125rem, 1.5): the hero's one line under the headline, `text-2`, at most 34rem.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.55): running text; measure 34rem (about 68 characters). Smaller steps: 0.9375rem, 0.875rem, 0.8125rem.
- **Label** (500, 0.8125rem): Copy, the video's controls, table heads, check heads. Tags at 500, 0.75rem with 0.01em tracking.
- **Mono** (400, 0.875em of its context): file names, hashes, commands, the source address, inline code.

### Named Rules
**The Condensed Voice Rule.** Radio Canada Condensed Bold sets the headline, the section titles, Get knobs's title and the footer column heads, and nothing else. Every other word, buttons and labels included, is Radio Canada.

**The Copyable Mono Rule.** Source Code Pro is only for things a visitor can copy or type: file names, SHA-256 values, the PowerShell line, build commands, paths and the source address.

**The Logo Face Rule.** The wordmark's face lives only in the logo files, outlined. It is never set as text on the page.

**The No Shouting Rule.** No uppercase labels, no letter-spaced kickers, no labels above titles. Headings are sentence case and start the content they head.

## Layout

The page is a stack of full-width bands on a 77rem content grid (1232px at the default size) with a `clamp(16px, 5vw, 64px)` gutter. The bands alternate ground and deep ground: the header and hero on graphite (one ground, no rule between them), Download for Windows and the four steps deep, How it works on graphite, Benchmarks deep, Questions on graphite, Get knobs deep, then the footer band. Bands pad `clamp(64px, 7vw, 104px)` on top and `clamp(64px, 6vw, 96px)` below; a section title sits `clamp(32px, 3.5vw, 56px)` over its content. The steps follow the downloads on the same ground and open closer under them.

Every section's title and content start on one left edge, the edge the hero headline starts on. Download for Windows keeps a narrow 48rem column on that left edge. Paired rows (a benchmark's claim beside its chart, a question group's name beside its questions) use a 20rem head column and a `clamp(40px, 4.5vw, 64px)` column gap, and are ruled: a strong rule opens the set, hairlines divide it. The hero is two columns, the pitch at 33fr beside the 16:9 video at 40fr, centred on each other.

Breakpoints are in em so larger text takes a narrower window's layout: 1024px (benchmark and question rows stack), 900px (the hero stacks and centres with full-width buttons; the steps and the signal path turn into vertical rails on the left; the step and signal subgrids switch on above it), 864px (the header's links take their own row), 768px (facts and footer take one column), 640px (each file's Download goes under its text, full width), 480px (header links spread edge to edge), 375px (header links step down a size), 360px (the headline steps down, the nav drops Download, Source becomes its mark). Short desktop windows (max-height 50em, then about 632 to 680px tall) shrink the headline, lede and header so the big button and the requirements stay in the first screen. Container queries handle the release stacks, the file rows, the code wells and the charts, so each adapts to its own width.

**The Touch Rule.** On coarse pointers every target is at least 44px tall: buttons, Copy, disclosures, header links and tools, the video's controls, the footer's link lists. A link inside a sentence keeps its line and gets an invisible hit area 44px tall centred on it, as one inline box that moves to the next line whole rather than breaking inside.

**The Paper Rule.** Print is the light scheme (theme.js switches to it for the print and back after). Fills that matter (bars, swatches, dots, fader caps, step 3's node) print exactly; solid buttons print as red outlines with red words; the footer drops its band for the black one-colour lockup on the page's own colours; screen-only controls and the reel stay off the paper.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. Depth is tone: panels (`raised`) stand a step lighter than their band in dark and white on the grey in light, wells (`sunken`) a step darker inside them, and bands alternate ground and deep ground. There are no drop shadows, glows, gradients or translucent glass anywhere.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Second Pixel** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px var(--action)`): the recommended file row's 2px cap red edge, drawn as an inset second pixel inside its 1px border so the row keeps its size. A border technique, not elevation; it is the only box-shadow on the page.

### Named Rules
**The Tone, Not Shadow Rule.** A surface is lifted by being a step lighter (or white in light) and edged by a 1px line, never by a shadow.

## Shapes

Every rectangle (button, panel, well, tag, frame, focus ring, bar end, legend swatch) takes `--radius`, which is 0. Lines are 1px throughout; `--hairline` (1px) is defined in `tokens.css` but no rule uses it yet, and its role is open until the shared brand token set is decided. Every round thing (step node, cap dot, fader handle, signal head, list dot in the filter chain) is a true circle. Round elements are sized in odd pixels (the 9px cap dot, the 33px step node, the 21px signal icons, the 7px signal head) so a 1px rule runs exactly through their centres; with larger text they grow, kept odd where the browser can round.

Icons are one inline sprite cut like the mark: 16px boxes, 1.5px strokes, butt caps and miter joins, round only where the thing is round. GitHub's mark keeps GitHub's shape and never turns red. The signal path's arrowheads and knobs's caret are the only diagonals; there the 45 degrees mean direction.

The favicon is the mark alone, filling its square: one SVG holding the brand's two marks, the graphite knob on light tabs and the ink knob on dark ones, switching with the system's light or dark setting. Browsers that can't switch an SVG favicon take `favicon.ico`, the mark on its graphite tile, which reads on any tab. The iPhone home-screen icon keeps its square graphite tile, since iOS fills transparency with black.

**The Cap Dot Rule.** The page's one graphic device is the cap dot: a 9px cap red circle, a knob cap seen from above, marking the items of a list (requirements, facts, questions). It grows with the visitor's text.

## Components

### Buttons
Red caps: solid or outlined, square, sinking 1px when pressed.
- **Shape:** square (0px), 1px border, 44px minimum height, 0 20px padding, Radio Canada 500 at 0.9375rem, icon gap 10px.
- **Primary:** cap red fill, words graphite in dark and white in light. Used for the hero's and Get knobs's big download, and for Releases on GitHub where there's no file to offer.
- **Hero:** 64px tall in every release state, at least 17.5rem wide. With a release it is the two-line download button: a 20px download arrow, the label at 1.125rem 600 ("Download knobs 1.0.0"), and a second line at 0.875rem 500 with the file and its size. Off Windows the label reads "Download for Windows" and the second line names the file without repeating Windows ("x64 installer (.exe) · 4.8 MB"); that is how the page says what knobs runs on. Without a file it opens the release with GitHub's mark, its one line at the same 1.125rem, at 500.
- **Secondary:** transparent with a cap red outline and red words; fills with cap red under the pointer. Used for each runnable file's Download in Download for Windows (installer and portable zip) and for Try again.
- **Quiet link:** the checksums file's Download is a plain link in `text-2` with the download icon, red under the pointer, never a third red button.
- **Try again:** beside Releases on GitHub where the stack is wide enough; wherever the two can't sit side by side (below 26.5rem of stack), it goes under the big button at a plain 44px height and 0.9375rem type, so the big one stays the one to press.
- **Hover / Focus / Active:** colour changes at 160ms on the page curve; the focus ring is 2px `--focus` at a 2px offset, square; pressed buttons move down 1px; pressed links and summaries dim to 0.72 opacity.

### Tags
- **Style:** 22px tall, 0 7px padding, 1px `line-strong` border, `text-2`, 500 at 0.75rem. Square.
- **State:** "Recommended" on knobs's main file takes the red outline and red words. "Pre-release" stays a plain grey tag, because red never warns.

### Copy and small controls
- **Copy:** a quiet outlined button (32px, 44px on touch; at least 5.75rem wide) in `text-2` with a `line-strong` border, red under the pointer. Copied, its icon becomes a check in ink with an ink-grey border for 1.8s, then returns.
- The **video's controls** take the same shape in label type: square and outlined on the page's ground, their edge and words red under the pointer.

### Navigation
- **Header:** on the hero band, no rule. The colour lockup at 32px tall on the left (light-ink version on graphite, dark-ink version on the grey; both are in the markup and CSS shows the one for the scheme on screen). Section links in `text-2` at 0.9375rem, no underline, red on hover. At the right end, Source (outlined, GitHub's mark plus "Source") and the light/dark toggle (a square icon button, a sun while dark shows, a moon while light shows), both 38px tall, 44px on touch.
- **Toggle behaviour:** dark until pressed, whatever the system's scheme; the choice is stored in `localStorage` as `knobs-theme` and a stored light is applied by an inline script in `<head>` before the first paint, together with the browser bar's `theme-color`. Without JavaScript the toggle is absent and the page stays dark; in forced colours it is hidden.

### Links
A link is its words on a 1px cap red underline at a 0.22em offset; the underline, not colour alone, says it's a link. Under the pointer the words turn red too. External links end in a small arrow icon kept on the last word. In the footer the underlines are white and thicken to 2px under the pointer.

### File rows and checksum disclosures (signature)
- **Row:** a square panel (`raised`, white in light) with a 1px hairline edge, 24px 28px 18px padding, a 16px gap between rows. The recommended file takes a 2px cap red edge (the Second Pixel). Title, description (34rem measure), then the file name in mono and its size, with the Download on the right; below 640px the Download goes under the text at full width.

- **Release states:** a stack shows one state at a time (checking, ready, error), each holding its button in the same place, so an answer arriving never moves what is under it; page.js eases the stack's height over 320ms and the new state settles in.

### Wells and code
Mono wells sit inside panels on `sunken`; the build commands sit on the band itself, so they take the panel tone (`raised`). Commands wrap between words under a 2ch hanging indent, with Copy in its own column (under the commands in a narrow well).

### Step rail
Four steps on one 1px `line-strong` rule, each opening on a 33px numbered circle: outlined, `text-2`, on its band's ground. **Only step 3's node is a red cap** (filled cap red, words on red), because step 3 is getting knobs running. Below 900px the rail runs down the left with each title level with its node. In forced colours step 3's node stays filled in the visitor's text colour.

### Signal path (How it works)
One 1px wire in `text-3` through four stages left to right (top to bottom below 900px), each marked by a 21px stroked icon on the wire, each stretch ending in an arrowhead. knobs's stage is the one panel: square, `raised`, a 1px cap red edge, hung under its red icon with a caret in its top edge pointing up at it. Inside, the filter chain is drawn rather than named: a `sunken` well with a row per filter, a red dot on a short wire, and a fader at its own value with a red cap handle.
- **The pass:** once, when the whole drawing is in view, one signal (a small cap head with a trail as long as it is fast) runs from the mic in ink into knobs, where each filter's fader runs up to its setting in red and settles back to grey, then leaves knobs in cap red through the cable to the apps, which light as it reaches them. The whole path holds lit for about 600ms and settles. About 3 seconds in all. It runs once, with no control to run it again; a drawing already on screen when the page loads stays lit and still.
- **Still:** with reduced motion, without script, in forced colours and in print, nothing moves, and the lit drawing is simply there.

### Charts (Benchmarks)
Each claim sits beside its evidence; the evidence is a real table or definition list that CSS draws as bars. Every bar is its value on a linear scale from zero, square-ended: knobs's bar cap red and set first, OBS's the unlit meter, each with its printed value (knobs's in ink at 600). A range draws its low end solid and the rest at 0.32 opacity. GPU is said in words, not drawn.
- **Lower is better:** said once for the footprint's four measures, which all run the same way, at the right end of the legend's line in label type (500, 0.8125rem) and `text-3`, taking its own line when the legend fills the row. It is for the eye only, and the table's caption says it to screen readers. Same delay and Same sound have none.
- **The sweep:** the first time a chart scrolls into view, one 720ms clip sweep on the page curve draws its bars from zero, at the same pace on every line. Charts already on screen, and every chart with reduced motion or in print, are simply drawn.

### Ruled rows (facts, questions)
No cards. Three facts sit in ruled columns, each opening on a strong rule with a cap dot at its start. Questions sit in three groups as ruled rows, the group name quiet on the left, each question marked by a cap dot centred on its first line with the answer hung under it, all open.

### The reel
The hero's video is the knobs reel: 24 seconds at 60 fps, a seamless loop of picture and sound, dark in both schemes (the brand's videos are dark only), its edges graphite. It sits square at 16:9 with no frame: on the dark ground it has no edge at all, and on the light one it stands as a graphite panel of its own. Nothing sits over it.
- **Files** (`site/media/`), made from the reel as delivered, with its sound from the reel's lossless master: `knobs-reel-av1.mp4`, AV1 (10-bit, CRF 35) with Opus at 128 kbps, 3.5 MB; and for browsers without AV1, `knobs-reel.mp4`, H.264 (High, no B-frames, CRF 21) with AAC at 192 kbps, 6.6 MB. Both are 1920 × 1080, exactly 1440 frames, in one-second fragments, with keyframes on frames 0 and 1312. The poster, `knobs-reel.webp` (26 KB), is frame 1312, the end card with every word in. All three decode to exact graphite at their edges.
- **Sound that tiles:** each file's sound is encoded from the master as three loops, and only the middle loop's packets are kept, with no priming and no edit list: 1200 Opus packets of 960 samples, or 1125 AAC frames of 1024. Played end to end, one copy's last packet runs into the next copy's first as it did in the encode.
- **Playing:** nothing loads until the reel is wanted. Then it plays muted, from frame 1312, so the poster turns into motion without a cut, only while it's on screen and the tab is showing. It waits for Play when the browser asks to save data or the visitor asks for reduced motion, the poster showing until then.
- **The loop:** where Media Source Extensions (or Safari's Managed Media Source) take an encode, page.js fetches it once and puts its header in, then its fragments again for every loop, end to end on one open-ended timeline, each copy trimmed to exactly its 24 seconds and put in 12 seconds ahead, two copies at most. Picture and sound run across the seam with no gap. Elsewhere, or if anything fails, the browser's own loop plays the same files, with the short gap at the seam it leaves.
- **Controls:** in a row 14px under the reel, on the right (centred below 900px); without JavaScript, the browser's own controls over it instead. Pause/Play with its word, the sound (a speaker with an x while muted, with waves while on) and Expand (four outward corners), each a square outlined button in label type on the page's ground, 36px (44px on touch), its edge and words red under the pointer. While the reel loads to play, the button already offers Pause. A visitor's own Pause stands until they press Play.
- **Screen readers** hear the figure's name and a description of what the reel shows, which says no more than the page does.
- **Expanded:** Expand opens the reel large in a modal `<dialog>` over the page, which stays in sight, dimmed under graphite at 80% in both schemes. The reel is as wide as fits at 16:9 inside a `clamp(24px, 5vw, 80px)` margin, with a row for the controls below it. The same `<video>` moves into it, so it plays on without a break; Expand counts as pressing Play. Its controls go with it, under it on the right as under the reel, in the dark scheme's roles on the dimmed page, and Expand becomes Minimize (four inward corners) in the same place, keeping the focus. Meanwhile the reel's place in the hero takes the page's ground. Minimize, Esc or a click on the dimmed page moves it back, focus still on the same button, Expand again. The page behind can't scroll. It fades in over 160ms and the frame falls into place, as new content does.
- The reel is left off the printed page.

### Footer band
Big and calm: a band of cap red deep (`band`) in both schemes, with every word white. The white one-colour lockup at 44px tall with the line under it, two link columns ("On GitHub", "On this page") under a band hairline, each headed in Radio Canada Condensed Bold, then the legal lines at 500. Inside it the page's colour roles take the band's own values, so links, rules, focus and selection read on the red without rules of their own. In forced colours on a light canvas the black one-colour lockup replaces the white; in print the band is dropped for the black lockup on the page's own colours. ### Motion and scheme switches
One curve, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`: 160ms for feedback (hover, copy, a disclosure opening, a chevron turning) and 320ms for structure (a release state settling, a stack resizing, things going out after the signal pass). New content settles in from 4px above with a fade: it falls into place, which the brand guide allows beside the app's rise, and opens down from the line that opened it. Beyond that only the charts' one sweep and the signal path's one pass move on their own. A scheme switch, whether by the toggle or for print, lands in one frame: an `is-switching` class turns every transition off for two frames, so no colour eases from the old scheme. Reduced motion turns off every animation and transition.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour from the knobs-brand tokens, and add a new one in knobs-brand's `tokens.json` with a value in both schemes, never in the site.
- **Do** use cap red (`--action`) for actions and for things that are knobs, and only for those.
- **Do** put graphite words on cap red in dark and white words on cap red deep in light and on the footer band, at weight 500 or more for small words.
- **Do** make the hero and Get knobs downloads solid red 64px buttons, each runnable file's Download an outlined red button, and the checksums file's Download a quiet link.
- **Do** keep step 3's node the only red cap on the step rail.
- **Do** underline every link in cap red (white in the footer).
- **Do** give every rectangle square corners (`--radius`, 0px) and make every round thing a true circle, sized in odd pixels where a rule runs through it.
- **Do** set the headline, section titles, Get knobs's title and the footer column heads in Radio Canada Condensed Bold, everything else in Radio Canada, and only copyable things in Source Code Pro.
- **Do** use the one curve with 160ms for feedback and 320ms for structure, and keep any moving thing to a single play with a still state for reduced motion, forced colours and print.
- **Do** give every target 44px on coarse pointers, and every link inside a sentence a centred 44px hit area as one inline box.
- **Do** apply a stored scheme choice before the first paint, and let a scheme switch land in one frame.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use red for an error, a warning, a pre-release, or a success; don't add green, amber or any status colour.
- **Don't** put brand ink on cap red (2.8:1).
- **Don't** add a second large field of red; the footer band is the one.
- **Don't** add shadows, glows, gradients or glass; lift a surface by tone and a 1px line.
- **Don't** round a rectangle's corners, or square off a round thing.
- **Don't** set the wordmark's face as text, or set body copy, buttons or labels in the condensed face.
- **Don't** add uppercase kickers or eyebrow labels above titles.
- **Don't** fade colours during a scheme switch, or repeat a page animation on its own; the signal pass and the chart sweep each play once (the hero video's loop is content, and always has its Pause, its sound and Expand).
- **Don't** change the light-mode chart colours to separate the bars by lightness; labels, order and values already carry the meaning.
- **Don't** colour GitHub's mark red; it keeps GitHub's shape and the ink colour.
